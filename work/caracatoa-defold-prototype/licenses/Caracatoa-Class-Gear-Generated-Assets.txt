Caracatoa class gear generator

The additional face, hair, armor, cloak, bow, staff, sword, shield, quiver and
axe geometry is generated by the Caracatoa project tool:
tools/generate-caracatoa-class-gear.mjs

The humanoid outfit bodies that the added geometry accompanies are from
Quaternius, Modular Character Outfits - Fantasy, available under CC0 1.0:
https://quaternius.com/packs/modularcharacteroutfitsfantasy.html

No WYD client, server binary, proprietary model, texture or animation is used
by these generated additions.
